## Composition
Light warm-gray world; authentic working surfaces (terminal request, editor
panels, render timeline, finished player) arranged as one connected pipeline
across the frame. Foreground action is always a single large readable surface;
context panels recede to edges. Final payoff (rendered film) sits at the end
of the causal path, same world, no scene cut to decoration.

## Camera
Restrained: opens tight on the request surface, then a single continuous
pullback + travel following a thin causal thread from stage to stage. Holds
during work (analyzing, rendering at 100%), travels only to bridge
destinations. No zoom-punch, no orbit, no shake.

## Identity
Product surfaces are real and specific (exact panel labels, real filenames,
real progress states). Flat fills, thin rules, small mono/type labels. Zero
neon/glow/gradient; color is flat and functional (red thread, green 100% bar).

## Anti-pattern
Nothing floating in a void; no card grid, no badges, no particles; motion is
causal (thread travel, progress fills, block reveals) not decorative.
